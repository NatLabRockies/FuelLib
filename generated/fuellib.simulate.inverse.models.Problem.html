fuellib.simulate.inverse.models.Problem

class fuellib.simulate.inverse.models.Problem(fuel, config, *, ignore_composition=False)

Bases: object

Static fuel data, objective, and constraints of the inverse design problem.

Precompute the per-component properties of the fuel.

Parameters:

  • fuel (Fuel) – Base fuel to which an addition is blended.

  • config (Config) – Optimization settings.

  • ignore_composition (bool) – Whether to ignore the original composition of the fuel (fuel.Y_0). If True, the blend is made entirely of the addition (additive mass fraction of 1). Defaults to False.
