Fuel Property Prediction Framework

FuelLib predicts the properties of fuels from their composition. Fuels such as synthetic aviation turbine fuels (SATFs) are complex mixtures, and measured property data are often limited. FuelLib combines group contribution methods (GCMs), measured data for pure compounds, correlations, and mixture rules to estimate the properties of a fuel without extensive physical testing. This helps fuel developers identify promising fuel compositions before committing to large-scale production.

FuelLib builds on Pavan B. Govindaraju’s Matlab implementation of the Constantinou-Gani GCM[1], and includes gas chromatography data (GCxGC) for various jet fuels from the National Jet Fuel Combustion Program[2] (NJFCP), the Air Force Research Laboratory[3][4] (AFRL), and Vozka et al.[5].

FuelLib’s property prediction methodology

FuelLib predicts the properties of a fuel in four steps.

  1. Fuels are represented as a collection of individual compounds based on their GCxGC data bins. GCxGC analysis reports the weight percent of each hydrocarbon family and carbon number in a sample (e.g., 5% C10 isoalkane). FuelLib assigns pseudo compounds for each bin. This is generally a single compound per bin, but users can choose to distribute the weight percent of a bin across multiple compounds. FuelLib includes a selection of common reference compounds, and users can provide compounds as SMILES strings as well. The weight percents of the compounds become the initial mass fractions, y0.

  2. GCMs or measurement data predict the properties of individual compounds, typically at 298 K and 1 atm. Measured properties of pure compounds are used when they are available (see Reference Property Data). Otherwise, the properties are predicted from the groups that make up each compound with one of the Group Contribution Methods.

  3. Correlations predict the properties of individual compounds at other conditions. The standard-condition properties are inputs to correlations for temperature-dependent properties such as density, viscosity, vapor pressure, and surface tension (see Individual component properties (fuellib.correlate.components)).

  4. Mixture rules predict the properties of the mixture. The compound properties are combined with mixture rules weighted by the mass or mole fraction of each compound (see Mixture properties (fuellib.correlate.mixture)).

Notation

A tilde denotes a molar property (e.g., \(\tilde{C}_{p,i}\) in J/mol/K and \(\Delta\tilde{H}_{v,i}\) in J/mol), while the same symbol without a tilde denotes the corresponding mass-based property (e.g., \(C_{p,i}\) in J/kg/K and \(\Delta H_{v,i}\) in J/kg). The subscript i denotes the i-th compound in a mixture. The group-contribution parameters (e.g., \(h_{v1k}\)) follow the notation of the original publications.

The Fuel Class

Fuel objects wrap around multiple GCMs, correlations, and mixture rules, and are the easiest way to access fuel properties. A fuel is created from the name of its GCxGC data file:

from fuellib import Fuel, Units

fuel = Fuel("posf10325")
temp = Units.Quantity(300.0, "K")

fuel.num_compounds          # number of compounds
fuel.comps.tc               # critical temperature of each compound
fuel.comps.rho(temp)        # density of each compound at 300 K
fuel.mix.rho(fuel.y0, temp) # mixture density at 300 K

Pass use_ref_props=False to use only GCM predictions, or data_dir to use custom fuels and reference compounds (see Adding Custom Fuels). All quantities carry units (see Units).

Core attributes (Fuel.<attribute>)

The core attributes hold the input data and composition of the fuel.

name

Name of the fuel.

data_dir

Directory containing fuel data.

gc_data

GCxGC data as a DataFrame.

components_data

Matched components data with reference compounds.

prop_data

Property data for the fuel/mixture, if available.

metadata

Fuel metadata, if available.

y0

Normalized weight fractions from the GCxGC data.

x0

Normalized mole fractions from the GCxGC data.

num_compounds

Number of components in the fuel.

comps

Components attribute for the fuel.

mix

Mixture attribute for the fuel.

Individual compound properties (Fuel.comps.<att-method>)

Fuel.comps is a Components object. Its attributes are properties at standard conditions or structural properties, which are computed the first time they are accessed. Its methods evaluate properties at a given temperature with the Individual component properties (fuellib.correlate.components), or convert between mass, mole, and volume fractions. Each returns one value per compound.

Attributes

tc

Critical temperature of the fuel components in K.

pc

Critical pressure of the fuel components in Pa.

vc

Critical molar volume of the fuel components in m^3/mol.

tm

Melting point of the fuel components in K.

tb

Boiling point of the fuel components in K.

hf_stp

Enthalpy of formation (at STP) of the fuel components in J/mol.

hv_stp

Enthalpy of vaporization (at STP) of the fuel components in J/mol.

vm_stp

Molar volume (at STP) of the fuel components in m^3/mol.

omega

Acentric factor of the fuel components (dimensionless).

ysi

Yield Sooting Index (YSI) of the fuel components (dimensionless).

dcn

Derived cetane number (DCN) of the fuel components (dimensionless).

gf_stp

Gibbs free energy of formation (at STP) of the components in J/mol.

lj_eps_by_kb

Lennard-Jones potential well depth divided by Boltzmann constant in K.

lj_sigma

Lennard-Jones collision diameter of the fuel components in m.

names

Reference compound names of the fuel components.

smiles

SMILES strings of the fuel components.

pelephysics_keys

PelePhysics species keys of the fuel components, if in the GCxGC data.

rdkit_mols

RDKit Mol objects for the fuel components.

num_c

Number of carbon atoms in each fuel component.

num_h

Number of hydrogen atoms in each fuel component.

mw

Molecular weights of the fuel components in kg/mol.

formulas

Molecular formulas of the fuel components.

hc_type

Hydrocarbon type of each fuel component.

fam

Hydrocarbon family codes for thermal conductivity.

Methods

rho

Calculate the density of each component at a given temperature.

nu

Calculate component kinematic viscosity.

mu

Calculate component dynamic viscosity.

cp

Compute specific heat capacity at a given temperature.

pvap

Compute vapor pressure.

pvap_antoine_coeffs

Estimate Antoine coefficients for vapor pressure of each compound.

vm

Compute molar liquid volume with temperature correction.

hv

Calculate enthalpy of vaporization adjusted for temperature.

d

Compute diffusion coefficients using Lennard-Jones parameters.

sigma

Calculate surface tension of each compound at a given temperature.

k

Calculate thermal conductivity at a given temperature.

t_flash

Calculate flash point of each compound.

mass2y

Convert mass of each component to mass fractions (yi).

mass2x

Convert mass of each component to mole fractions (xi).

x2y

Convert mole fractions (xi) to mass fractions (yi).

y2x

Convert mass fractions (yi) to mole fractions (xi).

y2v

Convert mass fractions (yi) to volume fractions (vi).

Mixture properties (Fuel.mix.<method>)

Fuel.mix is a Mixture object. Its methods take the mass fractions of the compounds, yi, and return a single value for the mixture with the Mixture properties (fuellib.correlate.mixture).

rho

Calculate mixture density at a given temperature.

nu

Calculate kinematic viscosity of the mixture.

mu

Calculate dynamic viscosity of the mixture.

pvap

Calculate vapor pressure of the mixture.

pvap_antoine_coeffs

Estimate Antoine coefficients for vapor pressure of the mixture.

sigma

Calculate surface tension of the mixture.

k

Calculate thermal conductivity of the mixture.

mw

Calculate the mean molecular weight of the mixture.

t_freeze

Calculate freeze point of the mixture.

t_flash

Calculate flash point of the mixture.

lhv

Calculate heat of combustion (lower heating value) of the mixture.

ysi

Calculate yield sooting index of the mixture.

smoke_point

Calculate smoke point of the mixture.

dcn

Calculate derived cetane number of the mixture.

aromatics

Calculate aromatics content of the mixture.

naphthalenes

Calculate naphthalenes content of the mixture.
